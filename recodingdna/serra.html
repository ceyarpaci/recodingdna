Serra

b. Istanbul — lives & works between Turkey and Portugal

[Bio — first paragraph. Serra Behar is a multidisciplinary artist born in Istanbul, living and working between Turkey and Portugal. Her practice spans sculpture, installation, and performance — shaped by a deep curiosity about the body, memory, and material culture.]

[Second paragraph. Behar holds degrees in scenery and puppet design, as well as a master's in sculpture from Mimar Sinan University. She further studied experimental design with animatronic techniques at Nottingham Trent University, UK.]

[Third paragraph. Her work employs unconventional materials — wires, ceramic, industrial debris, found objects — to construct immersive installations that challenge the boundary between the biological and the built, the personal and the collective.]

[Fourth paragraph. Recoding DNA marks a new chapter in her practice: a return to the hand, to pattern, to the act of threading as both poetic and conceptual gesture — connecting the azulejo traditions of Portugal with the science of biological inheritance.]
